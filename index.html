// FAQ

Questions we
hear most

Who is ShiftStack for?

Mid-sized organizations and public-sector teams in Canada that know AI matters but are held back by legacy systems, scattered data, or a workforce that isn't yet confident with AI tools.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

Rarely all at once. We start with an assessment, identify where AI adds value on top of what you already run, and modernize in phases — so operations keep running while the stack evolves.

How long does an engagement take?

Discovery typically takes 2–3 weeks. We aim for first measurable quick wins within 4–6 weeks of starting delivery, with larger migrations and training programs running in parallel phases after that.

How do you handle data privacy and security?

Privacy is designed in from day one. We work within your governance and Canadian privacy requirements, favour keeping sensitive data in your own environment, and include AI ethics and governance in every training program.

What happens after you leave?

We set up an internal AI Center of Excellence and train change champions in each department, so your team owns the tools, the skills, and the roadmap long after the engagement ends.
